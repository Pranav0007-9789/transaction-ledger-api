# Assumptions

1. SQLite is used as the local relational database to keep setup simple.
2. Account IDs and transaction IDs are UUID strings generated by the server.
3. New accounts always start with a zero balance.
4. Money amounts must be greater than zero and have no more than two decimal places.
5. The balance must never become negative.
6. A reference ID must be unique within an account; another account may reuse the same reference ID.
7. Transactions are immutable through the API; no edit or delete routes are provided.
8. All timestamps are generated by the server in UTC.
9. Pagination defaults to page 1 and 10 records per page, with a maximum limit of 100.
10. Authentication and authorization are outside the assignment scope because they are not specified in the brief.
11. Transfers use two transaction records, one debit and one credit, and commit them together with both balance updates.
